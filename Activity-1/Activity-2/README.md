# Activity 2 — Personal Branding
## Description

This activity focuses on developing a personal brand through the intentional use of color and typography. The design choices were selected to create a consistent, elegant, and professional visual identity.

## Color Palette

My personal brand uses three main colors:

- **#2C1E1E** — A deep brown shade that adds warmth, depth, and sophistication to the design.
- **#CBB7A3** — A soft beige tone that creates a calm and refined appearance while complementing the darker color.
- **#FFFFFF** — White provides contrast and clean space, helping the other elements stand out and keeping the overall design balanced.


## Typography

Different typefaces were selected for specific purposes to create visual hierarchy and maintain consistency throughout the personal brand.

### Logo Font — Callen

I used Callen as the primary font for the logo. Its distinctive and elegant appearance helps establish a recognizable visual identity.

### Logo Accent Font — The Seasons

For the accent font of my logo, I used The Seasons. It adds a decorative and sophisticated element that complements the main logo font.

### Body Font — Quicksand

Quicksand is used for the body text because of its clean and readable appearance. It makes supporting information easy to read while maintaining the overall style of the brand.

### Tagline Fonts — Brittant and Cormorant Garamond

To add an elegant and expressive quality to the tagline, I have used the fonts Brittany and Cormorant Garamond. Their combination creates emphasis while complementing the other typography used in the design.

### How the Color Palette and Typography Support the Personal Brand

The combination of the selected colors and typefaces creates a consistent and professional personal identity. The warm color palette gives the brand a sophisticated and welcoming appearance, while the combination of decorative and readable fonts creates visual hierarchy. These design choices help make the personal brand more recognizable, cohesive, and visually appealing.
