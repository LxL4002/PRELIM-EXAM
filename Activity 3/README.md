# Activity 3: Social Media Infographics and Mini Project Documentation

## Description

This activity focuses on creating a social media infographic and documenting the concept, design choices, and creative process behind the project. The infographic presents a technology-based solution to a serious and relevant community problem: flooding.

## Project Concept

The project is called **Smart Flood Alert**. It presents the idea of using modern technology to help communities prepare for and respond to flooding.

The infographic presents flooding as not only an environmental concern but also a threat to homes, health, families, livelihoods, and communities. It introduces an IoT-Based Smart Flood Early Warning System that uses real-time monitoring, risk analysis, and rapid alerts to help communities respond before flooding becomes more severe.
The system is designed to monitor flood-related conditions, analyze risks, and provide timely alerts so that people can take appropriate action before the situation becomes more dangerous. The concept shows how information technology can be applied to a real-world problem and contribute to community safety and disaster preparedness.

## Design Choices

### Color Palette

The infographic uses a combination of colors to communicate different ideas and create visual emphasis.

- **Dark and Medium Blue** — Represents water, technology, reliability, and the seriousness of the flood-monitoring system.
- **Yellow** — Represents warning, attention, and the need to be alert.
- **Cream/Off-White** — Provides a clean and neutral background that improves readability and balances the stronger colors.
- **Red** — Represents danger, emergencies, and high-risk situations.
- **Green** — Represents safety, protection, and a positive response to flood risks.

The combination of these colors helps communicate the seriousness of flooding while also emphasizing the role of technology in providing warnings and protection.

### Typography

The infographic uses three main fonts:

- **Anton** — Used for prominent headings and important information to make key messages noticeable.
- **League Spartan** — This font was used because its bold and condensed style makes the title stand out and immediately captures attention. It is then paired with simpler, clean fonts for the supporting text to keep the infographic readable and balanced.
- **Montserrat** — Used for supporting information because of its clean and readable style.


### Visual Elements

Icons and visual elements were used to make the infographic more engaging and easier to understand. These include:

- Clouds and raindrops to represent weather and rainfall
- Lightning to represent severe weather conditions
- Damaged homes to represent the effects of flooding
- Health protection to represent community safety
- Families to represent the people affected by flooding
- Economic losses to represent the financial impact of disasters
- IoT sensors to represent real-time monitoring
- Cloud data storage to represent the collection and management of information
- Risk analysis to represent the assessment of flood conditions
- Mobile alerts to represent communication and early warnings
- Alert-level indicators to show different levels of flood risk

These visual elements help communicate the information quickly and make the infographic more understandable for a social media audience.

## Creative Process

The creative process began by identifying flooding as a serious and relevant problem that affects communities, families, homes, health, and the economy. After identifying the problem, the idea of **Smart Flood Alert** was developed as a technology-based solution.

The next step was to identify the technologies that could support the concept, including IoT sensors, cloud data storage, risk analysis, and mobile alerts. These ideas were then organized into an infographic to show how technology could help monitor flood risks and provide timely warnings.

Colors, typography, icons, and other visual elements were selected based on their ability to communicate specific ideas and make the information visually appealing. The content was then arranged into a clear layout to make the infographic easy to understand and suitable for social media.

## How the Project Demonstrates the Use of IT

The Smart Flood Alert project demonstrates how information technology can be applied to address real-world problems. By combining IoT sensors, cloud technology, data analysis, and mobile communication, the concept shows how digital technologies can support disaster preparedness, risk awareness, and community safety.
